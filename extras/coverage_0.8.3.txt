BA_IMPOSSIBLE(what, num) is Tcl_Panic("bitarray: internal error in FUNCTION: WHAT NUM (FILE:LINE)").  It is used where the code cannot get, whatever the Tcl script does:
GetElem and PutElem (width other than 8, 16, 32, 64; the 1-bit case is handled before), fill (after "to" there are always enough words) and the end of BitarrayCmd (every case returns).
Two of the four are run by tests (fi-6.1 to fi-6.3, in a separate process, through the test build's getelem / putelem commands); the other two cannot be reached from Tcl.
extras/bitarray.nocoverage does not mark BA_IMPOSSIBLE lines: they are asserts, not gaps.
